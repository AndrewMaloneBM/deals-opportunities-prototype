How reduced commission Deals work

This limited-time Deal applies to selected products in selected markets. You pay a reduced commission rate when you list these products at or below the recommended Deal prices during the Deal period. When the Deal ends, your commission rate will return to the pre-Deal rate.

We’ll update recommended prices once a week to keep up with market competition. You’ll see price changes reflected via CSV.
